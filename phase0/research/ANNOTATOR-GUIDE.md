# Annotator guide: is this text really in your language?

Thank you for helping. You will get one spreadsheet for one language (for example `skr_Arab.csv` = Saraiki).
Each row is a short piece of text taken from a large public web dataset. Please read each one and fill in
the empty columns. Expect about 2-3 hours for 150 rows. Work at your own pace; it is fine to stop and continue.

## Language codes

## The four columns to fill

1. **Is this text written in the language of the file?** Write one of:
   - `yes`: all or nearly all of it is this language.
   - `mostly`: this language, but with a noticeable amount of another language mixed in.
   - `no`: it is mainly another language.
2. **If not, which language?** Only when you answered `no` or `mostly`: Urdu, Roman Urdu, English, Persian,
   Punjabi, Saraiki, Pashto, Sindhi, Arabic, or `other`. If unsure, write your best guess and add `?`.
3. **Is it real running text?** `yes` for normal sentences (news, stories, articles, posts). `no` if it is mostly a
   list, menu, keywords, spam, adverts, garbled characters, or reads like a bad machine translation. Write the reason.
4. **Notes** (optional): anything else, e.g. "Saraiki with Punjabi spelling", "mixed with Urdu", "religious text".

## Rules

- Judge the language, not the topic. Religious, political or adult content is still `yes` if it is in the language.
- Do not look anything up and do not use translation tools. Your own knowledge is what we need.
- Text may be cut off at the end, and line breaks are shown as ` / `. That is expected.
- Do not discuss specific rows with other annotators until you have finished your own sheet.
- If a row is offensive or upsetting, you may skip it: write `skipped` in notes.

## About you (once, on a separate sheet)

Name or pseudonym, languages you speak natively, where you grew up, and whether you were paid. This is only for
the papers' ethics section; your name will not be published without your permission.
